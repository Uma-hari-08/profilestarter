## LangGraph or CrewAI?

CrewAI is fine for simple role-based agent teams. For production workflows with
branching, retries and human escalation, I prefer LangGraph because state and
routing are explicit.

## How do you know a RAG system is working?

I measure retrieval and answer quality separately. If retrieval finds the right document but the answer is still wrong, the problem is the prompt or the model,not search.

## Do you train ML models?

My focus is the engineering side: integrating models, retrieval, orchestration
and evaluation. On my EdTech work a dedicated ML engineer trained the models.
